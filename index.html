Questions people ask

Are you the government?
No. Rahisi Services is an independent assistance business. Every official portal (KRA, eCitizen, SHA, HELB, NTSA) is free to use yourself — many people simply prefer to pay someone experienced to do it quickly and correctly. We charge for our time, like a cyber café, except we come to you on WhatsApp.
How does the wallet work?
Create an account (phone + a 4-digit app PIN), deposit from KSh 50 by M-Pesa STK push, and pay for any service in one tap. Leftover balance stays for next time — or withdraw it back to M-Pesa (min KSh 100) within 24 hours, always.
What is the refund policy?
If we can't complete the task you paid for, you get a full refund — instantly back to your wallet, or to M-Pesa if you paid directly. The receipt ID in your request history is your proof.
Terms & privacy
Terms: We provide form-filling and portal-navigation assistance. Government decisions, approvals and processing times are outside anyone's control and are not guaranteed. Abusive requests will be refunded and refused.

Privacy: We use your details only to complete the task you requested, then delete them. We never sell or reuse your information. Your app PIN is stored hashed — even we can't see it. You can delete your account and all data yourself from your profile, anytime.
